How to use the annotator

The loop

  1. Pick a bag in Bags. Review queue lists the scans the model is least sure about.
  2. Model proposals are orange, your selections magenta. Step through them with Tab, check each in 3D, toggle with Space, or press A to accept every proposal and then fix the exceptions.
  3. When every error in the scan is selected, press Enter: the scan is saved as complete and the next queue item opens.
  4. After 30 or more new scans, press Train (about a minute). Proposals and the queue then come from the new model.
  5. Export classified bag writes a bag you can inspect in Foxglove.

Complete and partial

Complete means every error in the scan is selected, so all other points are taught as clean. A scan with nothing selected can be complete too. Partial (S) keeps your selections but is not trained on; it is only used to check that the model finds what you selected. Scans written to the ledger by another tool carry no mark: they are trained on when they have enough labels (6 unless training.min_labels says otherwise). To confirm them, set the menu beside “Complete & next” to “next unconfirmed scan” and press Enter on each one that is fully labelled: it walks through the reviewed scans that are not marked complete, in timeline order. Leaving a scan with unsaved changes saves it, keeping its mark.

Bags

Add… lists every bag file under the bags folder (bags_root in the project's annotator.json, or --bags-root); a bag anywhere else can be added by pasting its path. Hover a bag for ✎ (give it a name of your own, or read it from another point cloud topic) and ✕ (take it off the list; labels and cache stay). The bar under each bag shows complete (green) and other reviewed scans, partial or unmarked (yellow). The full path shows when you hover. Bags with three echoes show and label the nearest return of each beam.

Training and the numbers

Two models are trained: range only (works on any bag) and with intensity (used when the bag has intensity). While training runs, the sidebar shows the stage and a learning curve; Charts… has the full view.

  • Cross-validation: the complete scans are split into five parts by time; each part is scored by a model trained on the other four.
  • Precision: of the points flagged, the share you had selected. Errors you never clicked count against it, so it is a lower bound.
  • Recall: of the points you selected, the share flagged.
  • Average precision: one number for the whole precision-recall trade-off; higher is better.
  • Reviewed since training: scans you saved after the installed model was trained. The model has never seen them, so this is the most honest check.

The proposal threshold slider sets what is shown as a proposal, what the accuracy numbers refer to and what an export flags. The default threshold next to Train is stored in the model.

Files

Manage files… lists every model set and every export with its date, size and contents. Delete what you no longer need, or put an older model back with “Use”. The label ledger and the scan cache are never touched from there.

Panels

Drag the bar between the sidebar and the views, or between the range image and the 3D view, to resize. Double-click a bar to reset it. Sidebar sections fold when you click their heading.
